## Expat life

- **Suvarnabhumi auto gates: 33 nationalities (UK yes, US no)** — Arrival e-gates now cover UK, Australia, Canada, Japan, most of the EU and others (33 total after +31); you need a prior visit on the same passport, visa-free entry, and a completed TDAC. US, China, Taiwan and India are out for now; first-timers and visa holders still use staffed counters. [Time Out Bangkok](https://www.timeout.com/bangkok/news/suvarnabhumi-airport-auto-gates-expand-to-33-nationalities-092226) · [Deep Arrival](https://deeparrival.com/news/bangkok-suvarnabhumi-egates-33-nationalities-september-2026/)
- **Visa-free stay cut to 30 days from 15 Sep** — The old 60-day exemption is gone for new arrivals: 60 nationalities (incl. UK/US) get 30 days; Mauritius/Seychelles 15; VOA shrunk to Azerbaijan, Belarus and Serbia. Pre-15 Sep stamps keep their original “admitted until” date; a further 30-day extension (฿1,900) may be possible at immigration discretion. [The Thaiger](https://thethaiger.com/guides/visa-information/thailand-visa-rules-2026-september-15) · [Thai Embassy Helsinki](https://helsinki.thaiembassy.org/en/publicservice/updated-tourist-visa-exemption-scheme-30-days-effective-15-september-2)
