Jargon: what is MCP? — MCP (Model Context Protocol) is a shared “plug socket” so an AI assistant can discover and use outside tools and data — calendars, files, browsers, company apps — without each vendor inventing a private integration. Think of the model as the brain and MCP as a standard way for that brain to pick up tools safely. The Grok SDK above talks to remote MCP; DevDay also floated “MCP Events” so automations start when something happens in a connected app. When a brief says agents are “calling tools,” MCP is often the wiring underneath.

UN General Assembly sits on the Iran sanctions veto — On 1 Oct the Assembly, under its veto initiative, examined the China–Russia veto of a US-backed panel of experts on Iran nuclear sanctions. Assembly president Khalilur Rahman said the debate cannot replace Council action. China’s Sun Lei blamed the US withdrawal from the 2015 deal and strikes on Iran; the EU’s Hedda Samson said no member should stop the Council functioning. A summary goes back to the Security Council. UN News

Ethiopia and Eritrea cut diplomatic ties — On 2 Oct Ethiopia said it was closing its Asmara embassy and ordered 10 Eritrean diplomats out within 48 hours; Eritrea severed ties in response. Same day Ethiopia declared an Egyptian embassy employee persona non grata (48 hours). Context: renewed fighting in the north — the most serious since the 2022 peace deal. A doctor in Alamata told reporters at least 52 civilians were killed in three days (local report). Explosions in Addis Ababa overnight were reported; cause and casualties were not confirmed. The AU Commission urged restraint; the UN warned strikes and road closures are disrupting aid. Hiiraan · Anadolu

Russia’s September toll, and the winter grid campaign — Al Jazeera (2 Oct): Ukraine’s military put Russian killed or wounded in September at 46,230, and 2,090 on 28 Sep — Al Jazeera says it cannot verify either side’s casualty claims. Kyiv says Operation Vivaldi has retaken about 176 sq km near Lyman; ISW’s net Russian gains for the year are put at about 148 sq km, against a Russian claim of about 5,300. Russia kept hitting energy sites. In the week to 1 Oct Ukraine says it downed 81% of 1,933 long-range drones, and Zelenskyy put the jet-powered Shahed interception rate at 55%. Ukraine is seeking about an extra $27bn. Al Jazeera

Sudan: US 90-day ceasefire idea, rejected in public — Jerusalem Post / The Media Line (2 Oct): US adviser Massad Boulos proposed a 90-day ceasefire then talks with the RSF. Adviser Amjad Farid, speaking for the army side, told Reuters the plan looks like a Libya-style partition and that the government’s demand — an internationally monitored RSF pullout from cities — was not taken up.
